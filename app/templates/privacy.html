Privacy and data

Junior Signals learns which kinds of signals are worth scoring higher from how people use them. Here is exactly what we record, why, and how to switch it off.

What we record

Your accountYour Telegram id, first name and username, watchlist, alert settings, and the alerts we sent you (so we never send one twice and can send a correction if we withdraw one).
RatingsYour Useful / Not useful taps, so the buttons show your choice.
Reading behaviourOn the dashboard: which signals were on screen, which you opened and for how long, which you scrolled past, and source links you clicked. In Telegram: Useful / Not useful taps. Also watchlist changes and the event-type filters you use.
CommentsAnything you type in a signal's comment box. Founders read these; they are not used to change scores and are never sent to the AI.

How reading behaviour is stored

Under a pseudonymous id, not your Telegram id: a code computed on our server from your account, the date and a secret key. It changes every day, your browser never sees or chooses it, and it cannot be connected to you without that secret. It is pseudonymous, not anonymous: we can recompute it, which is how /stop deletes your behaviour, and because your Useful / Not useful choices are also stored with your account (so the buttons show them), someone with access to our database and the secret could connect a day's reading to you. We don't. Each event stores only the time, the signal, its event type and ticker, and for reading time the number of seconds.

Raw events are deleted after {{ retention_days }} days. After that we keep only totals per event type.

Why

Totals per event type (how often each kind of signal is opened, read, clicked or rated useful) let the system propose changes to its scoring weights. It never applies them by itself: a founder reviews every proposed change with the numbers behind it, and only approved changes take effect. Alerts themselves are published automatically; your behaviour never decides whether a single alert is sent.

How to opt out

  • Dashboard: tick Don't record my reading behaviour under Alerts and save.
  • Telegram: send /tracking off to the bot.
  • Browser: if it sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we record nothing from it.{% if gpc %} Your browser is sending it now.{% endif %}
  • Delete everything linked to you: send /stop to the bot.

Opting out stops reading-behaviour recording. Your explicit Useful / Not useful ratings still count, without your identity, in per-event-type totals. Information only, not investment advice.
