TITLE: Example Copper Announces Strategic Investment by Big Copper Mining
VANCOUVER, British Columbia - Example Copper Corp. (TSXV: EXMP) ("Example" or the "Company") is pleased to announce that it has entered into a subscription agreement with Big Copper Mining Ltd. ("Big Copper") for a non-brokered private placement of 25,000,000 common shares at a price of C$0.50 per share for gross proceeds of C$12.5 million.
The issue price represents a 25% premium to the 20-day volume weighted average price of the Company's shares.
Upon closing, Big Copper will hold approximately 9.9% of the issued and outstanding common shares of the Company on a non-diluted basis.
Big Copper will have the right to nominate one director to the Company's board for so long as it holds at least 5% of the outstanding shares.
This is a fictional example document for testing the pipeline.
